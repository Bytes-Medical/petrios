01  TUE 6 OCT · 12:30–14:00 · In person
Acute asthma in children: recognition and escalation
With Dr Hannah Whitfield and Joanne Pryce

A case-based walk through a school-age child with acute wheeze, from
first assessment in the emergency department to deciding when to
escalate, finishing with a structured look at discharge.

Key points
 1. Grade severity on the whole picture (SpO2, ability to talk,
    respiratory and heart rate, work of breathing) and reassess after
    every intervention.
 2. A silent chest, cyanosis, poor respiratory effort, exhaustion or
    altered consciousness are life-threatening: call your senior and the
    anaesthetic/PICU team early.
 3. Before discharge, check inhaler and spacer technique, give a
    written asthma action plan and arrange early follow-up in primary
    care.

02  WED 7 OCT · 13:00–14:00 · Petrios Meet
Neonatal skin: what’s normal, what’s not
With Dr Priya Raman

A photo-led tour of common newborn skin findings, separating benign
transient rashes from presentations that need same-day senior review.

Key points
 1. Erythema toxicum neonatorum and transient neonatal pustular
    melanosis are common and self-limiting: reassure, and avoid
    investigating a well baby.
 2. Vesicles in a newborn mean HSV until proven otherwise: swab for
    PCR and discuss starting aciclovir with a senior without delay.
 3. Record the size and site of haemangiomas; periocular,
    “beard”-distribution or large segmental lesions need early referral.
